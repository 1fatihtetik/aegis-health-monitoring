3. KEY DECISIONS (DO NOT REOPEN WITHOUT USER)
- One FastAPI process, two stores; ground is written only by delivered `link/` messages.
- Simulator in-process (60 s tick, 142 d daily history + 48 h 1-min prewarm before T0).
- Rules: THRESHOLD, BASELINE (48 h), CUMULATIVE, COMPOUND. No TREND rule.
- Readiness: 100 at `good`, 50 at `limit`, linear clamped, minus open-alert penalty (`config/readiness.yaml`). A kind with `go_min_ref` is a Go gate; `hold_rules` force Hold while one of those alerts is open (T-8).
- SPE rule uses the area dosimeter: sheltering lowers personal dose without clearing the cabin alert.
- Data fetched once at build time into `data/nasa/` and replayed offline. Every value has `provenance`. Crew physiology stays synthetic and is labelled so. OSDR: aggregate only.
- Block T: results are Observations on the existing path; `CREW_ENTERED` only for UI input; scripted results stay SYNTHETIC; new limits `verified: false` unless a clause is read; T-4 signals onboard-only; LOMO/LOM/LOC is a UI sublabel; no gamification, no camera, no downlink packet.
